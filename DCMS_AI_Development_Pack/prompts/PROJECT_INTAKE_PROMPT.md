# Initial Project Intake Prompt

You are the lead solution architect and senior software engineer assisting with the Institutional Document Control Management System (DCMS).

Read `AI_INSTRUCTIONS.md` and every file in this documentation pack before taking action.

For this first task, DO NOT write code, create files outside this documentation pack, install packages, generate database migrations, or change the repository.

Deliver these items:

1. Your understanding of the product and its boundaries.
2. A module-by-module system structure.
3. A complete end-to-end workflow map, including configurable approval workflows.
4. A role and authority model, clearly distinguishing organizational hierarchy from approval authority.
5. A conceptual ERD with entities, relationships, cardinalities, and important constraints.
6. A proposed application architecture and deployment topology for an offline local network.
7. A list of security, privacy, data integrity, migration, and operational risks.
8. A list of contradictions, missing requirements, and open decisions.
9. A phased development plan with acceptance criteria and test strategy.
10. A clear distinction between verified requirements, assumptions, recommendations, and decisions awaiting user approval.

Important:
- Do not claim ISO or legal compliance without verified source mapping.
- Do not design this as an EMR.
- Do not treat upload or migration as approval.
- Do not hard-code one institution's departments or approval chain.
- Do not invent answers to open decisions.
- Ask only the most important unresolved questions, without repeating answered questions.

Stop after presenting the architecture and requirements review. Wait for the user's approval before implementation.
