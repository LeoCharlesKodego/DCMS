# ONP Three-Year Retention Schedule Proposal

**Status: Proposed for Project Owner review; not approved for activation.**

## Source basis

The ONP original guidelines, section 8, state: “Master and Authorized copies are generally retained 3 years or until rescinded/revised.” The same section gives a three-year periodic review interval. Annex 1 provides a per-document retention-period field. The source does not define a precise digital retention trigger or establish one schedule for every operational-record type. Annex 6 samples include both three- and five-year records, so those examples are not generalized here.

## Proposed schedule entry

| Field | Proposal |
|---|---|
| Record/document class | Master and Authorized controlled-document copies only |
| Duration | 3 years |
| Unit | Years |
| Trigger | Not proposed. Owner decision required before activation. |
| Basis | ONP original guidelines, section 8, as the recorded institutional source; legal applicability has not been verified |
| Review | At least every 3 years, or sooner when statutory requirements or procedures change, per the same source |
| Disposition | KMT review and documented disposition decision; no automatic deletion |
| Status | Inactive proposal pending owner decision and D-022 paper-versus-digital controls |

## Limits and questions for approval

- This proposal does not set retention periods for operational records, training evidence, or every document type.
- “3 years or until rescinded/revised” does not explicitly state whether three years runs from creation, release, supersession, or rescission. No trigger is recommended in this revision; owner direction is required.
- The original language concerns Master and Authorized copies. It does not establish that digital files may replace paper copies or the paper controls tracked under D-022.
- Legal, regulatory, and institution-specific applicability has not been verified.
- The proposal must remain inactive until the trigger, scope, source/legal basis, and paper/digital relationship are approved.

## Owner review response

On 2026-11-16, the owner directed: “Keep inactive; revise the proposal.” This revision removes the suggested trigger and leaves it for owner decision. The three-year period remains a proposal based on ONP source wording, not an active retention rule.
