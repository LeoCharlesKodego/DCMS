# DCMS User Training Guide (Draft)

**Status: Draft training material. Attendance and competency records are not yet collected by this guide.** Use an approved training environment and fictional or sanitized data. Do not place patient charts in DCMS.

## Shared principles

- A file upload or migration is not an approval.
- Approval, effective-date confirmation, release, distribution, and acknowledgment are separate records.
- Work only within your current role and organizational-unit assignment.
- Report incorrect metadata or duplicate candidates; do not silently replace a released file.
- Do not share account credentials or use another person's account to record a decision.
- Treat retained and distributed files according to ONP confidentiality and paper-copy procedures.

## KMT

1. Review document requests and official code components before assigning a code.
2. Register versions and follow configured workflows; do not treat a submitted file as approved.
3. Release only when the source-supported workflow and effective-date checks are complete.
4. Distribute the exact released version to the intended targets and set acknowledgment requirements.
5. Review recipient status; any acknowledgment exemption applies to one person and requires a reason.
6. Import source files into a migration batch, preserve source metadata, inspect duplicate candidates, and record verification evidence.
7. Register verified migration items only as unreleased drafts; never invent approval dates, approvers, owners, or revision labels.
8. Maintain external references and operational-record metadata only within approved procedures.
9. Review reports and audit history; do not edit audit evidence.

## Process Owners and unit document owners

1. Request a document record and provide accurate title, type, owner, and unit information.
2. Upload a draft as a new version and record the change summary for revisions.
3. Respond to assigned review tasks and provide a reason when returning a draft.
4. After approval, propose an effective date where the workflow calls for it; KMT confirmation and release remain separate.
5. Check that a distribution identifies the intended released version. Acknowledgment confirms receipt, not training or competency.

## QMR, committee, and reviewers

1. Open only assigned workflow tasks and confirm the exact document revision before deciding.
2. Record review or approval through your own account; do not approve your own submission where separation of duties applies.
3. Use a reasoned return or rejection when required and verify that the decision appears in the audit history.

## System administrators

1. Manage accounts, institution structure, and configuration within administrator permissions.
2. System administrator status does not grant document approval, distribution, migration verification, or records disposition authority.
3. Preserve offline recovery credentials and follow the documented two-person recovery process.
4. Do not run database migrations or restore backups without the approved change plan and verified recovery point.

## Training delivery checklist

- Introduce sign-in, password change, navigation, and role scope.
- Demonstrate document request, versioning, review, approval, release, and audit separately.
- Demonstrate distribution and self-acknowledgment; explain the limited one-person exemption.
- Demonstrate migration staging, duplicate review, and why imported files stay unapproved.
- Cover confidentiality, external references, holds, inactive retention proposal, backup/restore procedures, and escalation contacts after those policies are approved.
- Record session date, trainer, audience/roles, materials version, attendance, questions, and follow-up actions in the institution-approved training record system.
