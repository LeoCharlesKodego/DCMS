# Operational Records, Retention, and Disposition

## Scope

This module manages in-scope institutional operational evidence, not patient medical charts or clinical patient data.

Examples may include training evidence, internal audit records, equipment calibration records, inspection records, maintenance evidence, and controlled registers, subject to institutional policy.

## Retention configuration

A retention schedule should define:
- record type;
- retention duration and unit;
- trigger event (creation, closure, supersession, separation, or another approved event);
- legal or policy basis;
- responsible organizational unit;
- disposition method;
- approval authority;
- hold conditions.

## Disposition workflow

1. System identifies records approaching or reaching their disposition review date.
2. Authorized records officer reviews eligibility.
3. System checks legal hold, investigation, litigation, regulatory, and institutional restrictions.
4. A disposition request is created.
5. Authorized authority approves, rejects, or defers.
6. Approved disposition is executed by an authorized process.
7. Evidence and audit history are retained.

## Safeguards

- Date reached does not mean automatic deletion.
- Legal holds override ordinary retention expiry.
- Disposition requires approval and evidence.
- Preserve a disposition register.
- Applicable retention periods must be verified for the institution and record type.
